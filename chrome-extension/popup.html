Allowing a site only lets its page-declared WebMCP tools be listed. It does not give generic control of the page, and it does not share cookies or passwords. WebMCP is a proposed standard, and a page must opt in by registering tools.

Local site: localhost and 127.0.0.1 are allowed automatically in local mode, and tools are re-discovered when you click Allow, no page reload needed.
